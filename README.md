# KK Prompter: free teleprompter + camera/voice recorder

Read your script on screen while you record your **face + voice**, or **voice only**, on a phone or a laptop.
The text scrolls at the top of the screen, right under the front camera, so your eyes stay close to the lens.

**Open it here:** https://systechgd.github.io/kk-prompter/

- Free. No account, no ads, no watermark, no limit on script length.
- **Nothing is uploaded.** Your scripts stay in the browser on your device. A recording is saved only when you tap Save.
- Works offline after the first visit, and can be installed like an app.

---

## 1. Install on your phone (1 minute)

1. Open **https://systechgd.github.io/kk-prompter/** in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone).
2. Tap **🎥 Camera + voice** (or **🎙️ Voice only**), then tap **Allow** for camera and microphone.
3. Add it to your home screen:
   - **Android:** Chrome menu **⋮** → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
   - **iPhone:** **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen**.
4. From now on, open **KK Prompter** from the home screen. It opens full screen.

## 2. Use it on a laptop

Open the same link in Chrome or Edge and allow the camera and mic.
Make the window tall and narrow, and move it so the text sits just below the webcam.

Keys: `Space` play/pause the scroll · `R` record/stop · `↑` `↓` faster/slower · `Home` back to the top.
On a laptop, **Share / Save** opens a "Save as" window, so you can choose the folder directly.

---

## 3. Add your script

Tap **📄 Scripts**, then:
- **📋 Paste new**: paste any text and tap **Save script**. Or
- **📂 Open file**: pick a `.txt` or `.md` file (from Google Drive, Files, or a file you sent yourself on WhatsApp).

Scripts stay saved on that device. Tap one in the list to switch. Tap 🗑️ twice to delete it.

### Optional reading marks
Plain text works fine. If you add these marks, the prompter shows them clearly:

| Write this | You see | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `**🔊 START READING**` … `**🔇 STOP READING**` | only the text between them | everything outside is ignored (notes, visual plans) |
| `**word**` | word in yellow | stress this word |
| `/` | small `/` | short pause |
| `//` | green `‖` | long pause |
| `_(smile)_` | small green italic | tone cue, don't read it aloud |
| a new line | new line | take a fresh breath |

## 4. Set up the camera for a good picture

Do this once (**⚙️ Settings**). The app remembers it.

| Setting | Best choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame** | **Wide (more room)** | Uses the camera's full picture, so you can sit at a normal distance. "Tall 9:16" cuts the sides off. |
| **Quality** | **High** (try it first), else 1080p | High asks the phone for its sharpest camera mode. If the picture freezes or stutters, use 1080p. |
| **Preview** | **Whole picture** | You see exactly what is recorded. "Fill screen" crops the sides on screen only, which looks zoomed in. |
| **Zoom** (if shown) | the **lowest** value | Some phones zoom in by default. Slide it all the way left for the widest view. |
| **Lens** (if shown) | try each one | Some phones have a normal and a wide front lens. Pick the one that frames you best. |

The bottom of Settings shows the real camera size, e.g. `Camera: 1080×1440`. Taller than wide = portrait, which is correct.

**Placing the phone**
- Prop it at **eye level**, 40–60 cm away (about one arm's length). A stack of books works. A ₹200 phone tripod is better.
- **Face a window** or a lamp. Light on your face matters more than anything else for quality. Never sit with a window behind you.
- Clean the front camera lens with a soft cloth. A greasy lens makes everything look blurry.

**About quality (honest note).** A browser gets a plain camera picture. It does not get the extras of the phone's own Camera app
(HDR, night mode, beauty filters, stabilisation). With good light and a clean lens it looks good, but if you need the very best
picture, use the "best quality" setup below.

**Best quality setup:** record with the phone's **back camera** in its own **Camera app**, and show KK Prompter on a **laptop or second phone**
placed right behind or just above the phone, so you look near the lens while reading. Or use **🎙️ Voice only** in KK Prompter for the audio.

## 5. Record a take

1. Check the **green level bar** while you talk. It should reach about the middle. If it turns **red**, move back a little.
2. Press the **red button**. You get **3-2-1**, recording starts, and the text starts scrolling about 1 second later.
   - **Tap the text** to pause or play. **Drag** it to go back a line.
   - Too fast or too slow? Change **Scroll speed** in Settings between takes (start around 40–50).
3. Press the **red button** again to stop. Then choose:
   - **📤 Share / Save**: save to Files, Google Drive, or send it to yourself on WhatsApp.
   - **⬇️ Download**: save to the phone's Downloads folder.
   - **🗑️ Discard**: delete it and try again.

Made a mistake? **Don't stop.** Pause for a second, then say the whole line again. Cut the mistake later while editing.

**Sound tips:** keep the phone 30–60 cm from your mouth, or use earphones with a mic. Turn off fans if you can.
In a quiet room, turn **Reduce background noise** off for a more natural voice.

## 6. All settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Scroll speed | Reading pace. |
| Text size | Bigger = easier to read. 3–5 words per line is ideal. |
| Text area height | How much of the screen the text uses. Keep it in the top half, near the camera. |
| Background darkness | Dark band behind the text so it is readable over the camera picture. |
| Start scrolling when recording starts | On by default. |
| 3-2-1 countdown | On by default. |
| Mirror text | Only for a glass "beam-splitter" teleprompter rig. |
| Show pause marks | Hide `/` and `‖` if you prefer plain text. |
| Reduce background noise | Helps with fans and traffic. |
| Record | Camera + voice, or Voice only (audio file, with a big level meter on screen). |
| Camera | Front or Back. |
| Quality / Frame / Preview / Lens / Zoom | See section 4. |

## 7. Files

- File name: `kk-<script>-video-take2-2026-11-15-1532.mp4` (or `-voice-….m4a`).
- iPhones and newer Chrome save **MP4 / M4A**. Some Android phones save **WebM**. Most editors (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere) open both.

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Picture looks zoomed in | Settings → Frame **Wide**, Preview **Whole picture**, Zoom slider to the **lowest**, try another **Lens**. |
| Picture is blurry or dark | More light on your face, clean the lens, Quality **High**. Check the camera size at the bottom of Settings. |
| Video stutters | Quality **1080p** or **720p**. Close other apps. |
| "Camera/mic blocked" | Browser site settings → allow Camera and Microphone for this site, then reopen the app. |
| Black screen on iPhone | Close the app fully and reopen. Make sure no other app is using the camera. |
| Screen turns off while reading | The app keeps the screen awake while the camera is on. If it still sleeps, increase the phone's screen timeout. |
| Video is sideways | Lock the phone in portrait before opening the app. |
| New version not showing | Close and reopen the app twice (it updates in the background). |
